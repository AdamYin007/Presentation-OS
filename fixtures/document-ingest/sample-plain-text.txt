Q3 2026 Business Review

REVENUE PERFORMANCE
Total revenue for Q3 2026 reached $42.5M, representing 18% year-over-year growth. The enterprise segment drove the majority of growth with 24% increase, while SMB segment grew 12%.

KEY METRICS
Customer acquisition cost decreased by 8% due to improved marketing efficiency. Monthly recurring revenue stabilized at $3.6M with a churn rate of 2.1%. Net revenue retention improved to 112%.

PRODUCT UPDATES
Released version 3.2 with enhanced dashboard analytics and improved mobile experience. Customer satisfaction score (CSAT) increased from 4.1 to 4.5 out of 5.0.

RISK FACTORS
Increased competition in the mid-market segment. Supply chain delays affecting hardware-dependent product lines. Currency fluctuation impacting international revenue by approximately 3%.

NEXT STEPS
Expand sales team in APAC region. Launch new self-service onboarding flow. Invest in customer success to reduce churn below 2%.
