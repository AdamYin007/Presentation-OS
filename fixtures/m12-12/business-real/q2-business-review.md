# Quarterly Business Review: Q2 2026

## Executive Summary

This quarter, our company demonstrated strong performance across all key metrics. Revenue grew 15% year-over-year, customer satisfaction improved to 92%, and we launched three new product features that have been well-received by users.

## Financial Performance

### Revenue Growth
- Total revenue: $48.2M (up 15% from Q2 2025)
- Recurring revenue: $36.1M (75% of total)
- New customer acquisition: $12.1M
- Expansion revenue from existing customers: $8.3M

### Profitability
- Gross margin improved to 78% (up from 75%)
- Operating expenses: $28.5M (down 3% due to efficiency gains)
- EBITDA: $9.2M (margin of 19%)

## Customer Metrics

### Acquisition & Retention
- New customers acquired: 342 (up 22% from Q1)
- Net retention rate: 118%
- Churn rate: 2.1% (lowest in company history)
- Average deal size: $35,400 (up 8%)

### Satisfaction
- Net Promoter Score (NPS): 62 (up from 55)
- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT): 92%
- Support ticket resolution time: 4.2 hours average

## Product Updates

### New Features Launched
1. **Advanced Analytics Dashboard** — Real-time data visualization with custom KPI tracking
2. **Team Collaboration Tools** — Shared workspaces with real-time editing
3. **API v2 Release** — Improved performance and expanded endpoint coverage

### Roadmap Highlights
- AI-powered insights engine (Q3)
- Mobile app redesign (Q3)
- Enterprise SSO integration (Q4)

## Strategic Initiatives

### Market Expansion
- Entered two new geographic markets (APAC and EMEA)
- Partner program launched with 15 strategic partners
- Conference presence increased to 8 major events

### Operational Excellence
- Implemented automated CI/CD pipeline
- Reduced infrastructure costs by 12%
- Team grew from 120 to 145 employees

## Outlook

We are well-positioned for continued growth in H2 2026. Key priorities include scaling our sales organization, accelerating product innovation, and deepening customer relationships. We expect revenue growth of 18-20% for the full year 2026.
