Q3 delivered strong revenue growth of 18% YoY, driven by enterprise segment expansion and successful product launches in the AI analytics line. However, churn increased to 4.2% from 3.1%, requiring immediate attention to retention strategy.

## Customer Health & Retention

### Churn Deep Dive

The 4.2% churn rate is above our 3.0% target. Root cause analysis reveals:

1. **Price sensitivity** (35% of churn): Mid-market customers migrating to competitors
2. **Feature gaps** (28%): Missing integrations with Salesforce and ServiceNow
3. **Support experience** (22%): Response time SLA breaches
4. **Product issues** (15%): Stability incidents in August

### Action Items

- Launch mid-market pricing tier by end of Q4
- Prioritize Salesforce integration in engineering roadmap
- Implement proactive support outreach for at-risk accounts
