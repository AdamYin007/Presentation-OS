## The Problem

Companies lose $2.4M annually on average due to poor data visibility. Decision-makers spend 60% of their time **finding** data and only 40% **using** it.

### Current Pain Points

- Data scattered across 15+ disconnected tools
- Reports take 3 days to compile manually
- No real-time visibility into key metrics
- Mobile access is an afterthought

## Our Solution

Aurora Analytics unifies your entire data stack into a single, intuitive platform.

### Key Capabilities

1. **Connect**: One-click integrations with 200+ data sources
2. **Analyze**: AI-powered natural language queries ("Show me Q3 revenue by region")
3. **Visualize**: Drag-and-drop dashboards with 50+ chart types
4. **Collaborate**: Share insights with comments, annotations, and scheduled reports
